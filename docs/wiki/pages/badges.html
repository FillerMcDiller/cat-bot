Badges

Badges are part of the cosmetics system and are equipped from /cosmetics. They show up in /profile and in cosmetic display helpers.

How they work

  • Owned cosmetics are tracked on the profile as a comma-separated list.
  • Badge equips are saved to the profile's equipped badge field.
  • Some cosmetics are starter/default unlocks; others cost Kibbles.
