Cat Competition — Summer 2026

Submit your cat drawing for the community competition. Deadline: June 20, 2026 (23:59:59 UTC).

How to submit

  1. Use the slash command /catcomp in Discord to get the website submission link.
  2. Open the website form and provide the required images and fields.
  3. Provide the cat name and optionally comma-separated attack names.
  4. After submission, staff will review entries; winners will be announced and contacted by DM.

Prize list

  • 1st: 100,000 Kibble, profile cosmetic "CatComp Winner 2026", 30 Celestial Packs
  • 2nd–3rd: 50,000 Kibble, 15 Celestial Packs
  • 4th–15th: 10,000 Kibble, 5 Celestial Packs

All winners also receive the profile badge "CatComp 2026".

Notes

Submissions are handled via the website form. Staff will review entries and contact winners by DM. If you need the bot to also archive submissions, ask to enable that integration.
