Commands

The bot exposes a large command set. This page focuses on the main player-facing commands and the admin/setup tools visible in the code.

Core player commands

/inventory /cats /items /shop /packs /gift /trade /battlepass /profile /cosmetics /prism /rain /vote /fight

Collection and economy

  • /inventory shows cats and items.
  • /cats gives a filterable cat browser.
  • /items and /shop manage consumables and Kibble purchases.
  • /packs opens pack rewards.
  • /gift transfers cats, Kibble, rain minutes, or packs.

Progression and style

  • /battlepass shows quests and rewards.
  • /profile shows stats, showcase, and cosmetics.
  • /cosmetics buys and equips badges, titles, colors, effects, and showcase slots.
  • /prism shows and crafts prisms.

Setup and admin
