Questions

Quick answers for the competition page.

What does the bot command do?

/catcomp opens this page so people can submit their entry in one place.

Is this a real upload form?

Yes. It posts the entry directly to the bot endpoint, which DMs the owner with the files and details.

What files are required?

The base cat image, an enchanted version, and a small emoji image. Everything else is optional metadata.

What happens after submit?

Staff gets the DM immediately and can review the entry without any extra copy/paste step.
