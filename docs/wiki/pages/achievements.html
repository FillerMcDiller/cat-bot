Achievements

Achievements are loaded from the aches_data JSON and shown on the profile and achievements screens. The bot also awards some achievements automatically as actions happen.

Common sources

  • Catch milestones and fast catches.
  • Battle wins and fight-related events.
  • Packs, gifts, rains, votes, and cosmetics activity.

Profile integration

The profile page counts unlocked achievements and shows a progress summary. Some achievements are used as requirements for cosmetics.
