Cattlepass

The battlepass is seasonal progression stored on the profile. It awards XP, cats, packs, and rain minutes through quests and level rewards.

Quest flow

  • Each day/session, the bot refreshes catch, misc, extra, and vote quests.
  • Quest progress is tracked on the profile and updated when cats are caught.
  • Bonus progression can be affected by active buffs and vote reward state.

Rewards

Battlepass rewards include cat packs, cat rewards, rain minutes, and extra rewards after the main track is finished.

Reminder setting

The command also lets players toggle battlepass reminders, which are DM-based and can be switched on or off from the command view.
