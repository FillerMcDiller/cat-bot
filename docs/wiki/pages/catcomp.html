Cat Competition — Summer 2026

Submit your cat drawing for the community competition. Deadline: June 20, 2026 (23:59:59 UTC).

How to submit

  1. Use the slash command /catcomp in Discord or open the submission portal directly.
  2. Open the website form and fill out the entry with the required images and fields.
  3. Provide the cat name and optionally comma-separated attack names.
  4. Submit the form. The bot DMs the owner with your entry and attached images.

Prize list

  • 1st: 100,000 Kibble, profile cosmetic "CatComp Winner 2026", 30 Celestial Packs
  • 2nd–3rd: 50,000 Kibble, 15 Celestial Packs
  • 4th–15th: 10,000 Kibble, 5 Celestial Packs

All winners also receive the profile badge "CatComp 2026".

Notes

Submissions go straight from the website to the bot endpoint, which forwards them to the owner by DM with the three required images and the entry metadata.
