Trading

Trading covers both direct gifts and the interactive trade flow. The bot supports transferring cats, Kibble, rain minutes, and packs between players.

Gift rules

  • Cat gifts use the actual cat instance list when needed, so favorited or adventuring cats are protected.
  • Gifts of 5 or more cats trigger a 20% tax choice with a pay-or-evade prompt.
  • Kibble, rain minutes, and packs can also be transferred.

Trade flow

The interactive trade command uses accept/decline states and can be cancelled by either side before it finishes.
