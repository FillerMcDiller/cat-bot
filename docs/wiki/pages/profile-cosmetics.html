Profile / Cosmetics

The profile page shows your cat stats, achievements, cosmetics, and showcase settings. Cosmetics are controlled through the profile and the cosmetics command.

Cosmetic categories

  • Colors
  • Badges
  • Titles
  • Effects
  • Showcase slots

Profile fields

The profile stores equipped cosmetic IDs plus summary counters for cats, catches, achievements, battlepass progress, and other visible stats.

Commands

/profile /cosmetics
