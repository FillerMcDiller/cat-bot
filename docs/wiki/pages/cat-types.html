Cat Types

Cat types are data-driven in type_dict. Higher weights mean the type is more common in normal spawns. Cats with weight 0 exist for packs and special systems but do not spawn naturally.

Spawnable types

Fine, Nice, Good, Rare, Wild, Baby, Epic, Sus, Zombie, Brave, Rickroll, Reverse, Superior, Trash, Legendary, Mythic, 8bit, Chef, Jamming, Corrupt, Professor, Water, Fire, Candy, Divine, Alien, Real, Ultimate, eGirl, TV, Donut

Special types

The Christmas cats also exist in the master type list, but have zero spawn weight: Santa, Elf, Snowman, ChristmasTree, Gingerbread, Cocoa, and Present.

Battle notes

The battle stats table gives each type its own HP, damage, weakness, and ability list. Weaknesses matter in the fight system and are part of the type identity in the code.
