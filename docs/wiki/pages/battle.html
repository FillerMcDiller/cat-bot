Battle System

Fights use a type-based battle engine with per-type HP, damage, weakness, and abilities. Decks and battle state are managed through Discord UI views.

Battle flow

  • Choose a deck and select cats.
  • Confirm or decline a challenge.
  • Pick abilities, switches, and battle actions from the UI.
  • Resolve turns until one side wins.

Deck tools

The battle cog supports deck save/load/rename/delete tools so players can keep multiple loadouts for different matchups.
